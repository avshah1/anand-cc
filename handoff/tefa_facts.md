# TEFA (Texas Education Freedom Accounts) — program facts

Source: Texas_TEFA_Voucher_Program_Spring_2026.docx (Claude-generated summary Eryn shared in Slack; **NOT independently validated by Anand** — verify before citing in any external doc).

## What it is
Texas's first K-12 voucher/ESA program. Created by SB2 (2025), run by the Texas Comptroller. ~$1B year-1, enough for ~90,000–100,000 students for 2026-27.

## Timeline
- Applications: Feb 4 – Mar 31, 2026 (deadline extended by court order from Mar 14).
- 274,000+ applications ("national record for year-one demand"). ~247,000 eligible, ~25,000 ineligible.

## Priority tiers (statutory)
- **Tier 1:** disability + household ≤500% FPG (+siblings). **All awarded round 1.**
- **Tier 2:** household ≤200% FPG (~$66k for family of 4). **Oversubscribed → awarded by RANDOM LOTTERY** (state law). Vendor Odyssey ran it; State Auditor + EY observed; sequential RNG position; awards in order until funds exhausted.
- **Tier 3:** 201–500% FPG. **Shut out year 1.**
- **Tier 4:** everyone else. **Shut out year 1.**

## Awards
- Round 1 (Apr 22): ~42,600 Tier 1 notified.
- Round 2 (May 4-6): 53,000+ Tier 2 notified.
- (Jack's Slack numbers: Tier 2 ~51,000 awarded, ~20,000 waitlisted.)

## Award amounts
- Private school: **$10,474/child** (85% of statewide avg per-pupil).
- Disability/IEP at private school: up to **$30,000**.
- Homeschool: $2,000. 504-only: standard $10,474.

## Rules relevant to research
- Approved private schools must be TEPSAC/TEA accredited, operating ≥2 yrs, and **must administer a nationally norm-referenced assessment in grades 3-12**.
- **Grades 3-12 TEFA participants at private schools MUST take an approved norm-referenced/state assessment each year; parents must report results to Odyssey** → potential test-score outcomes.
- Odyssey application platform allowed an **OPTIONAL pre-lottery private-school target** (no bearing on lottery) → potential first-stage if enough families listed Alpha.

## The identification structure (Jack's read)
1. **TEFA Tier-2 lottery** = clean random voucher offer, BUT treatment = "got a voucher," not "enrolled at Alpha." Alpha's tiny TX market share → tiny first stage. Lottery alone ≠ Alpha effect.
2. **The Alpha-specific play:** if Alpha campuses are oversubscribed by TEFA-awardees, **urge Alpha to randomize its own offers** → clean Alpha-enrollment experiment run entirely off Alpha internal data (interested list → random offer → enrollment for takers).
3. **Excess demand concentrated at** Alpha *sports academies* + maybe Brownsville — campuses with tuition near the $10k award (awardees are low-income; $10k won't buy the expensive campuses).
4. **Outcomes:** Alpha "losers" scatter to other private schools (not in public admin data) → measure AP take/scores, SAT/ACT, NSC college enrollment; plus the mandatory norm-referenced tests reported to Odyssey.
5. **Pre-lottery preference subsample:** if enough families optionally listed Alpha, lobby Odyssey to share data (applications, pre-lottery preference, lottery results, enrollment, test scores) → a second design with a real first stage.
