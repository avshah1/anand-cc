## The setup
DREAM is launching a federally-funded **Early Head Start / Head Start** program:
- Early Head Start launches Jul 6 (birth–2, home-based, socialization 2x/mo, 60 kids).
- Head Start launches Aug 24 (3s and 4s).
- 230 new students total.
- Head Start grant **expands 4-yo capacity from 36/yr → ~120/yr**.
- Sits on top of existing **UPK pre-K lottery since 2015** (36 four-year-olds/yr = two classrooms of 18).

## Two research clocks
- **Backward-looking** (the near-term paper): historical UPK lottery 2015→present. Oversubscribed → admitted vs non-admitted comparison on kids already in K-12.
- **Forward-looking** (panel-seeding, not near-term): new Head Start cohorts. Meaningful outcomes 5–25 yrs out. 60 EHS kids. Treat like Legenda / Boston pre-K long-run playbook — instrument consent now, don't expect a fast paper.

## Open asks worth raising (gaps beyond the existing action items)
1. **Eligible non-admit count, not just applications.** "Applications" ≠ eligible lottery participants. Need eligible-non-admits-who-entered-the-lottery as the denominator. (Ask Michael/Sunny.)
2. **Substitution / counterfactual = the whole ballgame.** Lottery-losers get *other NYC pre-K*, not nothing (Kline-Walters 2015 substitution problem). Notes say "Dream can use ATS to check whether non-enrolled applicants attended other NYC pre-K programs." Push on the ATS **match rate** before committing.
3. **Historical lottery integrity.** Was UPK 2015+ a clean random lottery or points/priority-weighted (siblings/zone)? Head Start NOW uses points (poverty, siblings, homelessness, foster care). If historical UPK had priorities too → need tie-breaker structure (DA propensity-score territory).
4. **K-12 outcome linkage feasibility, not just existence.** Outcomes need NYCDOE admin data. Are these kids traceable in NYCDOE, or do they scatter across charters/districts? Attrition out of NYC is the silent power-killer. (Leverage old Blueprint Gates/Walton NYC-match infra language.)
5. **Don't over-invest in forward EHS arm yet.** Panel-seeding play; instrument consent now, near-term deliverable is the backward UPK lottery study.
